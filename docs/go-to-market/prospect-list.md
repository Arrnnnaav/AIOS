## DISCOVER — pull the full cohort from these (free/public)
- **NCUA Credit Union Locator** mapping.ncua.gov + **Call Report data** — every US CU filterable by assets/members/state, with FTE counts.
- **FDIC BankFind Suite** banks.data.fdic.gov/bankfind-suite — every US bank by assets (<$50B)/state/charter + financials. Public holding cos → **SEC EDGAR 10-K / DEF 14A** name officers.
- **NAIC** content.naic.org + **state DOI licensee lists** — licensed carriers/agencies by state/line.
- **NAMIC** namic.org — mutual insurers (the EMC/Acuity/NJM/Donegal profile).
- **Big "I" / IIABA** independentagent.com — independent agencies.
- **ICBA** icba.org — self-identified community banks (lean back office).
- **CLM** theclm.org · **PLRB** plrb.org · WCI — TPAs/claims BPOs + the adjusters/examiners who are the end users.
- **HFMA** hfma.org · AAHAM — healthcare RCM shops + ops leaders.
- **ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer** projects.propublica.org/nonprofits — state-chartered CU 990s name execs + comp.

## The cohort query (turn into a discovery filter)
> US **credit union / community-or-regional bank (<$50B) / P&C-or-WC carrier /
> independent agency / claims TPA-BPO / healthcare RCM shop** · headcount
> **~200–5,000** (or a back-office unit that size inside a larger org) · in a
> **regulated, examined** vertical (NCUA/FDIC/state DOI/HIPAA) · with a back
> office of **processors/examiners/adjusters/MSRs on thick-client Windows
> line-of-business software** · showing pain: **turnover, exam/audit exposure, a
> recent merger or core-system migration, or a stated digital-adoption/enablement
> initiative.** **Boost:** lean regional with no big in-house cloud-security team →
> the no-egress story removes the security review that blocks cloud tools.

## ENRICH — where the name + title lives (per sub-segment)
- **Credit unions:** org Leadership page · state-chartered CU 990s (ProPublica) · NCUA call report (FTE) · LinkedIn title search.
- **Banks:** public holding cos → SEC DEF 14A/10-K named officers · private → About/Leadership + FDIC FTE · LinkedIn.
- **Carriers (mutual):** NAMIC + company Leadership page (rarely SEC) · LinkedIn `("VP Claims" OR "VP Underwriting Operations" OR "Compliance Officer") AND <carrier>`.
- **Agencies:** Applied/Vertafore (AMS360/Epic) user-group rosters + Big "I" chapters · LinkedIn.
- **Claims TPAs:** CLM/PLRB member & speaker lists · public TPAs → SEC · LinkedIn.
- **Healthcare RCM:** HFMA/AAHAM chapter leadership + conference speakers · Leadership page · LinkedIn.
