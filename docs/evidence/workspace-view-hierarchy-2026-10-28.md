# Workspace view hierarchy

28 October 2026. Read-only design references: [Apple Panels](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/panels) describes a compact floating surface for related controls; [Apple Segmented Controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/segmented-controls) distinguishes related views from top-level navigation; [Microsoft Copilot on Windows](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/windows) documents quick view beside the full app; [Raycast Quick AI](https://manual.raycast.com/ai/quick-ai) keeps one-off work in the entry window; [HeyClicky's site](https://www.hiclicky.com/) describes hotkey-led screen help. These informed the hierarchy, not copied assets or code.

Baseline local screenshots at 500 px: `output/playwright/chat-before-2026-10-28.png`, `plan-before-2026-10-28.png`, and `brain-before-2026-10-28.png`. The previous Chat empty state centered a large orb above three equal starter cards; Plan placed disabled actions over a blank page; Brain put memories, company knowledge, privacy, accounts, readiness and statistics in one scroll. An object-valued statistic rendered as `[object Object]`. The seeded screenshot server lacked a Company Brain, so the prior Brain view also displayed unusable Company controls and repeatedly received a 400 from the private-source endpoint.

The revised Chat opening is left-aligned with a shorter, direct prompt and the existing three choices. An empty Plan has one primary next action: Describe a goal returns to Chat and focuses its composer; with a goal, Draft plan uses the existing plan action. Brain has three in-page views: Personal, Company and Setup. The Company view shows an unavailable message when the local knowledge store is absent. Setup contains privacy, accounts, readiness and numeric statistics; object-valued statistics are omitted rather than printed as JavaScript object strings. All model and policy actions keep their existing API paths.

After screenshots: `output/playwright/chat-after-2026-10-28.png`, `plan-after-2026-10-28.png`, `brain-personal-after-2026-10-28.png`, and `brain-setup-after-2026-10-28.png`. Screenshots remain local because Setup can display account readiness. The focused Edge interaction test passed (1 passed, 53 deselected; `.tmp/pytest-shell-tabs-interactive`). It checked Plan-to-Chat focus, Brain section selection and focus, unavailable Company state, Setup controls, no `[object Object]`, and 500 px horizontal fit. Full suite and independent review results follow after their gates.
