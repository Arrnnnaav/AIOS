================================================================================
PART 1 — THE CONVERSATION
================================================================================

USER
  I want a 10-slide investor pitch deck for GhostCursor. And I want you to
  build it yourself, in PowerPoint, while I watch each step happen.

MODEL
  Before I propose anything: who is reading it, and in what setting?

USER
  Accelerator reviewers and early-stage investors. They'll click through it
  themselves, no narrator. Technical enough to care how it works, not reading
  code. Assume one pass and a few seconds per slide.

MODEL
  Then every slide has to land on its own. What is the product, in one line
  you'd actually defend?

USER
  A runtime that turns human intent into safe, verified work inside the
  software people already use. Customer-facing version: tell it what you want
  to accomplish, and it helps you do it directly in the app you're already in.

MODEL
  What separates that from a chat assistant with screenshots, and from an
  autonomous computer-use agent? Reviewers will have seen both.

USER
  It sits between them, and that's the whole point. A chatbot sees one
  screenshot per turn and tells you what to do. An autonomous agent takes
  your mouse and hopes. GhostCursor runs a continuous
  perceive → decide → show → verify loop: it watches the live UI through
  accessibility APIs, works out the next action, shows you where it is acting,
  and then independently checks the result actually happened.

MODEL
  "Independently checks" — what does that mean concretely? That is the claim I
  would push on if I were reviewing.

USER
  A separate signed process opens the saved file and confirms the effect. It
  does not take the adapter's word for it. That distinction has already paid
  for itself: in a 255-action run the adapter was correct and the verifier was
  wrong, and we found it because they disagreed. A system that trusted its own
  report would have called that a pass.

MODEL
  Do you have usage numbers, revenue, pilots or a closed round?

USER
  No. Pre-launch. No users, no revenue, no signed pilot, no raise closed.
  Do not invent any of that, and do not imply it with vague wording like
  "early traction". Slide 6 is engineering evidence instead — what actually
  runs and what was measured. That is a stronger claim at this stage anyway,
  because it is checkable.

MODEL
  Then what may slide 6 assert?

USER
  Only measured facts:
    - One signed 255-action PowerPoint plan completed 255 of 255, with every
      action independently verified from the saved file.
    - Excel's sheet-insert control was measured exposing InvokePattern with a
      RuntimeId stable across 100 of 100 observation cycles.
    - Four signed application plugins exist; three ship data-only.
    - Everything is local. No telemetry, no network, no cloud sync.
  If a number is not in the repository's evidence directory, it does not go on
  a slide.

MODEL
  Founders slide — names, roles, credentials?

USER
  Name and role only. Leave the credential line as a single short placeholder
  I will fill before it ships. Do not write a bio you cannot source.

MODEL
  Business model and revenue — one story or several?

USER
  The architecture is modular on purpose, and that is the business model. Core
  runtime plus application access plus intelligence modules plus knowledge
  plus authority plus collaboration. A customer need selects modules and that
  composition becomes a product package. Four we have already shaped:
  B2C learning, Developer Pro, Enterprise Onboarding, Enterprise Support.
  Pricing shape is not decided — say the packaging is modular, do not print a
  price.

MODEL
  Fund utilization — amount and split?

USER
  Not decided. Show the shape of what the money buys, not a number: reliability
  and verification depth, plugin coverage, and the enterprise trust surface.
  I will add the figure myself.

MODEL
  Why now, and what compounds over time?

USER
  Why now: accessibility APIs plus small local models are finally good enough
  to run this loop on a laptop without shipping anyone's screen to a server.
  What compounds: reliable application-state understanding, a plugin
  ecosystem, a verified workflow library, outcome-verification data, and the
  loop where a human resolution becomes an approved skill.

MODEL
  Last one. What must the deck avoid?

USER
  Do not make it look like a generic corporate template. Dark, technical,
  restrained. Diagrams instead of bullet paragraphs wherever something is a
  relationship. And restrained motion — animation should reveal or direct
  attention, never decorate. If a slide reads fine still, it ships still.

MODEL
  Understood. Settled brief below.


================================================================================
PART 2 — THE SETTLED BRIEF
================================================================================

PRODUCT OR TASK
  A 10-slide investor pitch deck for GhostCursor, built autonomously through
  the signed PowerPoint plugin while the user watches each step.

AUDIENCE
  Accelerator reviewers and early-stage investors. Self-guided reading, one
  pass, a few seconds per slide. Technical but not reading code.

DESIRED OUTCOME
  Ten slides a reviewer understands unnarrated, covering the seven required
  sections, saved to the approved path, every slide verified from the saved
  file rather than from the adapter's own report.

CONTENT
  Slide order:
     1. Title — product line and the customer-facing sentence
     2. Problem
     3. Value proposition — the position between chatbot and autonomous agent
     4. Founders
     5. Business model — modular composition into packages
     6. Traction — engineering evidence only
     7. Revenue model — packaging shape, no price
     8. Fund utilization — what it buys, no figure
     9. Why now and what compounds
    10. Close and ask

  TRUE, may be stated:
    - "A runtime that turns human intent into safe, verified work inside the
      software people already use."
    - Perception is UIA first, then OCR on captured pixels. No VLM in the
      shipping path.
    - Executes only signed, digest-bound plugin plans. One human approval binds
      plan, plugin version, target window and output path.
    - Every action is re-authorized immediately before it runs and verified
      afterwards by a separately signed read-only process.
    - Measured: a 255-action plan completed 255/255 with independent
      per-action verification.
    - Measured: Excel's sheet-insert control exposes InvokePattern, RuntimeId
      stable 100/100 across observation cycles.
    - Four signed application plugins; three data-only.
    - Local-only: no telemetry, no network, no cloud sync.
    - It shows where it is acting rather than acting invisibly.
    - Modular composition: core runtime + application access + intelligence
      modules + knowledge + authority + collaboration.
    - Package shapes: B2C Learning, Developer Pro, Enterprise Onboarding,
      Enterprise Support.
    - Compounding assets: application-state understanding, plugin ecosystem,
      verified workflow library, outcome-verification data, human-resolution
      to approved-skill loop.

  MUST NOT be asserted, in any wording:
    - Any user, revenue, retention, growth or waitlist number.
    - Any named customer, pilot or partnership.
    - Any raise amount, valuation or committed capital.
    - Any team size or hiring claim.
    - Any founder credential beyond name and role.
    - Softened versions of the above ("early traction", "growing interest").

DESIGN DIRECTION
  Dark, technical, restrained. One accent colour used sparingly. Two font
  families at most, strong title/body hierarchy. One idea per slide.
  Whitespace over density. Diagrams over paragraphs for anything relational.
  No stock photography, no clip art. Consistent spacing, corner radius and
  icon weight across all ten slides.

STRUCTURE
  Prefer these shapes over bulleted text:
    - Problem: a friction or before/after diagram
    - Value proposition: three-way comparison (chatbot / GhostCursor / agent)
    - Business model and revenue: metric or module cards
    - Traction: measured-evidence cards, not a growth chart
    - Fund utilization: proportional breakdown, unlabelled by amount
  Speaker notes on every slide.

MOTION
  Motion must explain. Permitted: progressive reveal of a list, a process
  advancing, attention directed to one figure. One transition family for the
  whole deck. If a slide reads correctly without animation, it ships without.

CONSTRAINTS
  PowerPoint build 16.0.20326.20112. Output written to the approved path only,
  created and never overwritten. Local machine only. The run should be
  watchable rather than instant: pointer movement and step labels are part of
  the deliverable, not overhead.

AVAILABLE PLUGIN
  ghostcursor.powerpoint (candidate, quarantined pending release signing).
  Compose only capabilities present in its signed capability and operation
  registries. If something needed is absent, say so rather than inventing it.

NON-GOALS
  - No mouse or keyboard synthesis.
  - No action outside the approved PowerPoint window.
  - No file access outside the approved output path.
  - No invented metrics, customers, funding or credentials.
  - No deck that needs a narrator to make sense.

OPEN QUESTIONS
  Resolved in the conversation above. Two placeholders remain for the user to
  fill by hand before the deck ships, and the model must leave them as short
  visible placeholders rather than guessing:
    - Founder credential line.
    - Fund utilization figure.

ACCEPTANCE
  - Exactly 10 slides, in the CONTENT order.
  - Speaker notes on every slide.
  - Every claim traces to a TRUE item above.
  - No number appears that is not sourced from the evidence directory.
  - Each action verified independently from the saved file.
