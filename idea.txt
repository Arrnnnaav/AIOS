PRODUCT OR TASK
  A 10-slide investor pitch deck for GhostCursor, built autonomously by
  GhostCursor itself through the signed PowerPoint plugin.

  The deck is both the artifact and the demonstration: the product builds its
  own pitch while the user watches each step happen.

AUDIENCE
  Early-stage investors and accelerator reviewers. Technical enough to care
  how it works; not reading code. Assume 3-5 seconds of attention per slide
  and one pass, no re-reading.

DESIRED OUTCOME
  Ten slides that a reviewer understands without narration, covering the
  seven sections the submission form requires, saved to the approved output
  path, with every slide verified against the saved file.

CONTENT
  Required sections, in this order:
    1. Title
    2. Problem Statement
    3. Value Proposition
    4. Founders
    5. Business Model
    6. Traction
    7. Revenue Model
    8. Fund Utilization
    9. Why now / moat
   10. Close and ask

  Facts that are TRUE and may be stated:
    - Windows-first desktop runtime; perception is UIA first, then OCR on
      captured pixels. No VLM in the shipping path.
    - Executes only signed, digest-bound plugin plans. One human approval
      binds plan, plugin version, target window, and output path.
    - Every action is re-authorized immediately before it runs, and verified
      afterwards by a separately signed read-only verifier process.
    - Measured: one 255-action PowerPoint plan completed 255/255 with
      independent per-action verification (docs/evidence/).
    - Measured: Excel's sheet-insert control exposes InvokePattern with a
      RuntimeId stable across 100 of 100 observation cycles (docs/evidence/).
    - Local-only. No telemetry, no network, no cloud sync.
    - It shows the user where it is acting rather than acting invisibly.

  Facts that are NOT established and must NOT be asserted:
    - Any user, revenue, retention or growth number.
    - Any named customer, pilot, or partnership.
    - Any funding amount raised or committed.
    - Any team size or hiring claim.
    See OPEN QUESTIONS.

DESIGN DIRECTION
  Dark technical, restrained. One accent colour used sparingly for emphasis.
  Two font families at most, with a clear title/body hierarchy. One idea per
  slide. Whitespace over density. No stock-photo filler, no clip art.
  Diagrams over paragraphs wherever a relationship is being explained.
  Consistent spacing, corner radius and icon weight across all ten slides.

STRUCTURE
  Title slide, then one section per slide in the CONTENT order above.
  Prefer these slide shapes over bulleted text:
    - Problem: a before/after or friction diagram
    - Value proposition: a three-part comparison
    - Business model and revenue: metric cards
    - Traction: a chart, only if real numbers exist (see OPEN QUESTIONS)
    - Fund utilization: a proportional breakdown
  Speaker notes on every slide.

MOTION
  Motion must explain, never decorate. Permitted purposes: revealing a list
  progressively, showing a process advancing, directing attention to one
  number. One transition family for the whole deck. No effect chosen because
  it looks impressive. If a slide reads correctly without animation, it ships
  without animation.

CONSTRAINTS
  Application: Microsoft PowerPoint, build 16.0.20326.20112.
  Output: the approved path only; the file is created, never overwritten.
  Privacy: local machine only; nothing leaves the device.
  Time: the run should be watchable, not instant. Pointer movement and step
  labels are part of the deliverable, not overhead to optimise away.

AVAILABLE PLUGIN
  ghostcursor.powerpoint (candidate, quarantined pending release signing).
  Only capabilities present in its signed capability and operation registries
  may be composed. If a needed capability is absent, say so in OPEN QUESTIONS
  rather than inventing one.

NON-GOALS
  - Do not synthesise mouse or keyboard input.
  - Do not act in any application other than the approved PowerPoint window.
  - Do not read or modify any file outside the approved output path.
  - Do not invent metrics, customers, funding or team facts.
  - Do not produce a deck that requires a narrator to make sense.

OPEN QUESTIONS
  These must be answered by the user before a plan is proposed. Ask them;
  do not guess.
    1. Traction: is there any real usage, pilot or waitlist number to show?
       If not, slide 6 should present engineering evidence instead
       (measured runs, verification results) and say so plainly.
    2. Founders: names, roles, one credential line each.
    3. Fund utilization: amount being raised and intended split.
    4. Revenue model: chosen pricing shape, or is it still open?
    5. Is there a named design reference or existing brand palette to match?

ACCEPTANCE
  - The saved .pptx contains exactly 10 slides in the CONTENT order.
  - Every slide carries speaker notes.
  - Every claim on every slide traces to a fact in CONTENT or to a user
    answer in OPEN QUESTIONS.
  - No slide contains a number that is not sourced.
  - Each action verified independently from the saved file, not from the
    adapter's own report.
